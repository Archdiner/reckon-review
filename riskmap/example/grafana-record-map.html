How to read this

  • Size is lines changed in the window — how much change the record had to explain.
  • Colour is the share of mechanism questions about those changes that the commit messages answer outright. It is an absolute share, not a rank.
  • A border marks a region changed in the last 3 months.
  • Hatched grey means the estimate is too thin to colour — fewer than 4 scorable commits, or a 95% interval wider than ±15 points. 3 of 64 regions here. A map that coloured those would be inventing confidence it has not earned.

What this is not

This is a map of what the record explains. It is not a map of what anyone understands, and the difference is the point: a written record cannot be evidence that a person understood a change, which is the central finding of the study behind this measurement. It contains no information about individuals — no authorship, no ownership, no activity — and none was collected.

Method

For a seeded sample of up to 15 commits per region, mechanism questions are generated from the diff alone by the same generator the product ships, then the commit message is scored against them by a second model that never sees the code. A tripped separation guard aborts the run rather than warning.

Body density here is 52.5% of the last 200 commits, above the 10% floor below which no map is drawn.

Calibration

Coverage here is comparable across the areas of this repository. A position against the study's 711-record corpus is in the JSON alongside this page, computed against the substantive-record subset — the like-for-like set, since only substantive commits are scored here.
